- [x] (fuzz-rng-low-bits) The Markdown harness's seeded fuzz was degenerate — 6 distinct inputs, not 5000 — commit ee90c66 (no plan file; tier `trivial`). `SeededLCG.nextInt(upperBound:)` reduced the **low** bits of a power-of-two-modulus LCG (`Int(next() % UInt64(upperBound))`). Bit *k* of such a generator has period at most 2^(k+1) — the low *k* bits of the recurrence depend only on the low *k* bits of the previous state, forming their own LCG mod 2^k sealed off from everything above — and the differential fuzz draws characters with `nextInt(upperBound: alphabet.count)` from an 8-entry alphabet, so that draw had period **8**. Measured directly against the shipped constants and seed `0x5EED_1234_ABCD_9876` rather than taken from the filed item: the low-bit `% 8` stream is `[5,0,7,2,1,4,3,6]` repeating; the high-bit stream is `[3,4,6,7,6,3,3,7,3,0,2,7,…]`; **distinct fuzz inputs over 5000 draws went 6 → 4740**. So the `md-link-scan-quadratic` differential oracle — the thing that proves the memoized link scan byte-identical to a naive reference — had been proving six strings for its whole life, invisibly, because everything was green. Fix is `Int((next() >> 33) % UInt64(upperBound))`; `>> 33` rather than the equally standard `>> 32` only because that is the shift the item probe-confirmed, and deviating would have invalidated its recorded evidence for no gain. **The open question the item flagged is closed with evidence:** it warned that a genuinely random corpus might surface a real pre-existing `parseLink` defect, and that finding one would be a find rather than a reason to revert — so the fixed fuzz was run out-of-tree against HEAD's renderer *before* any repo edit: ALL TESTS PASSED, 0 tree and 0 render mismatches across all 4740 inputs. There is no such defect. **Correction to the filed item, measured:** it claims "`% 48` (the length draw) is skewed by the same mechanism". It is not — 48 = 16·3, and while the mod-16 part does have period 16, the mod-3 part consumes higher bits with a long period, leaving the length draw's marginal distribution indistinguishable between the two reductions (48 distinct values, counts 86–128 low vs 83–129 high, over 5000 draws). The entire collapse came from the alphabet draw alone, whose modulus 8 is a pure power of two; the fix helps the length draw not at all. Also adds the guard that makes this durable, because a prose warning on `nextInt` cannot fail: `check(distinctInputs.count > 4_000, "…the corpus is not degenerate")`, threshold chosen to sit well below the measured 4740 (the shortfall is genuine birthday-paradox collision — `length == 0` alone recurs ~1/48 of the time) while still being ~790× above the degenerate 6. **Mutation-checked:** reverting only the reduction makes that one assertion FAIL with exactly `(6 unique)` while both differential assertions stay green — the whole bug in one line of output, and proof the guard is not vacuous.
